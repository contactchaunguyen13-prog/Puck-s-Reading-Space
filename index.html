About this space

I don't always struggle to read. Sometimes, I just struggle to stay. The words are there. My eyes move across the page. But they don't always hold me. So I created Puck's. Not to fix anything. Just to make it easier to stay a little longer.

How it works

Steady Flow
Moves gently through the page, one section at a time.

Gentle Emphasis
Softly emphasizes the beginning of words to support smoother reading.

Soft Breaks
Creates soft pauses to make longer text easier to read.
